Generative AI limitations

Three ways a foundation model's answer can be wrong without anything being "broken."

Hallucination
Confident, fluent output that is factually incorrect or fabricated — the model predicted a plausible-sounding token sequence, not a verified fact.
Knowledge cutoff
The date after which a foundation model has no training data, and therefore no built-in knowledge of events.
Bias (in this context)
A skew in a model's outputs that traces back to a skew already present in its training data.
Mitigations to know: grounding the model in real, current data instead of relying on memory (RAG — Domain 3), and keeping a human in the loop for high-stakes decisions.
