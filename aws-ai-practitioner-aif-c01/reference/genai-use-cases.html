Generative AI: good fit vs. poor fit

The litmus test: can a person review the output before it causes harm, and does the task tolerate "good enough" over one exact answer?

Good fits

  • Drafting marketing copy or emails (human edits before sending)
  • Summarizing long documents for a quick skim
  • Conversational chatbots for general Q&A
  • Generating first-draft code for a developer to review
  • Personalizing product descriptions at scale

Poor fits

  • Calculating exact interest, tax, or payroll amounts
  • Real-time safety shutoffs with no time for review
  • Anything requiring a fully auditable, reproducible decision trail
  • Tasks where a wrong-but-plausible answer causes immediate harm
One-line filter: review-tolerant + creative/subjective → good fit. No time to review + needs exactness → poor fit.
