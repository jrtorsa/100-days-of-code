Quick reference

Map of the 5 AIF-C01 exam domains

Source: Official AWS Exam Guide (PDF). Print this page or bookmark it: it's the reference you'll come back to in every lesson.

Domain Weight What it covers
1. Fundamentals of AI and ML 20% Basic terminology (AI, ML, deep learning), types of learning, the ML model lifecycle.
2. Fundamentals of Generative AI 24% Foundation models, tokens, prompts, embeddings, GenAI risks (hallucinations, etc.).
3. Applications of Foundation Models 28% Prompt design, RAG, fine-tuning, Bedrock, SageMaker, picking the right service for a use case.
4. Guidelines for Responsible AI 14% Bias, fairness, explainability, transparency, AWS tools for responsible AI.
5. Security, Compliance, and Governance for AI Solutions 14% IAM applied to AI, data encryption, model governance, regulatory compliance.
Key exam facts: 65 questions (50 scored, 15 unscored), 90 minutes, scaled score 100–1000, passing score is 700.
