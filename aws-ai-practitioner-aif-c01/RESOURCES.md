# AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) Resources

## Knowledge

- [Exam Guide (official PDF): AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01)](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/pdfs/aws-certification/latest/ai-practitioner-01/ai-practitioner-01.pdf)
  The authoritative source: the 5 domains, their weightings, exact task statements, and the list of in-scope/out-of-scope AWS services. Use for: confirming the exact scope of every lesson before writing it.
- [AWS Skill Builder: AWS Artificial Intelligence Practitioner Learning Plan](https://skillbuilder.aws/learning-plan/G8ENMJ5QBE/aws-artificial-intelligence-practitioner-learning-plan/SU2A1EJM1A)
  AWS's own free, structured learning path, organized by domain, with video and knowledge checks. Use for: the main structured course, domain by domain.
- [AWS Skill Builder: Exam Prep — AI Practitioner (AIF-C01)](https://skillbuilder.aws/category/exam-prep/ai-practitioner-AIF-C01)
  Official exam-prep content (steps 1–3 are free; the pretest and expanded question bank are paid). Use for: review at the end of each domain.
- ["AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) – Full Course" — Andrew Brown / ExamPro, on freeCodeCamp](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WZeZZ8_W-M4)
  Free ~15-hour video course covering all 5 domains with service demos. Very well regarded as a standalone pass-oriented resource. Use for: spoken/visual explanation when a concept needs more context, and console demos.
- [Tutorials Dojo — AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) Exam Guide](https://tutorialsdojo.com/aws-certified-ai-practitioner-aif-c01-exam-guide/)
  Widely used, well-maintained third-party study guide with service comparison tables. Use for: condensed domain/topic breakdowns and comparing similar services (e.g. Rekognition vs. Comprehend vs. Bedrock).

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/AWSCertifications](https://reddit.com/r/AWSCertifications)
  Active, moderated subreddit dedicated to AWS certifications, with recent AIF-C01 exam-experience threads. Use for: calibrating real question style, and troubleshooting specific confusing topics with people who already took the exam.

## Gaps

- Skill Builder's most complete hands-on labs sit behind the paid plan. While budget stays free, this will be compensated with the AWS Free Tier console for the few times hands-on practice is needed (e.g. trying a prompt in Bedrock).
- No free exam simulator of quality equivalent to the official one has been identified yet; the paid official pretest remains the most reliable option closer to exam day.
